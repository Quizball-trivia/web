# QuizUp reference collection

Added 26 trimmed segments and the complete local recording to Sound Lab under `QuizUp · reference`. These are comparison materials, not a new free-to-use sound pack.

## Source findings

- Local input: `Quizup - Sound Effects.mp3` in the canonical web project; 98.127528 seconds.
- Matching-title upload: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIRYE-FJucg — “Quizup - Sound Effects”, Mr. Noronha - VGM, approximately 99 seconds. The description states that the uploader does not own the music and credits Glu. Title/duration are consistent, but no waveform or byte match to this upload was established.
- Archive: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mrBTA9_ysBQ — Hellession, “QuizUp 1.0 + 2.0 - Entire Soundtrack and all sounds”. Its published chapters put 1.0 Sounds at 03:15 and 2.0 Sounds at 20:57. This is an additional listening reference, not the source asserted for the local montage.
- Public archive listing: https://www.letsplayindex.com/video/quizup-10-20-entire-soundtrack-and-all-sounds-hellession-mrBTA9_ysBQ

## Processing and use

The source recording was already in the workspace. Its audio was split at pauses of at least 0.7 seconds; short closely spaced hits remain grouped. Excerpts retain 40ms pre-roll and 120ms tail, with 3ms entrance and 15ms exit fades, and are converted to mono 44.1kHz PCM WAV. No synthesized replacement audio was used. Exact original cue names and game-action identities are unverified, so neutral numbers and source timestamps are shown.

The original local file is copied unchanged for full-recording playback. All clips and the full montage can play to completion; Stop and page-hide cancellation still apply. References are available as preview assignments, labelled with their source in exported presets, and excluded from `quizball-sound-pack.zip`. No live mode wiring changed.
