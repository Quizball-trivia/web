# QuizBall Semrush tracking setup

These files are the approved, repeatable keyword sets for QuizBall's international SEO campaign.

## Targets

| Market | Search engine | Device | Location | Language | Import file |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | Google | Mobile | United Kingdom | English | `uk-mobile-google-en.txt` |
| Spain | Google | Mobile | Spain | Spanish | `spain-mobile-google-es.txt` |
| Mexico | Google | Mobile | Mexico | Spanish | `mexico-mobile-google-es.txt` |

Keep the existing Greece/Greek target until its history is intentionally retired. Do not replace it: Semrush warns that changing the original target deletes its ranking history.

The keyword sets mix high-level category terms, intent-specific formats and current QuizBall topic clusters. `quizball` is included only as a branded baseline; weekly reporting should separate it from non-brand terms.

## Tags to apply after import

- `core`: football quiz/trivia category terms
- `format`: Career Path, Club Badges, guess-the-player and multiplayer intent
- `club`: club-specific quiz intent
- `brand`: QuizBall only
- `uk`, `spain`, or `mexico`: market tag

Review after four complete weeks. Keep keywords that gain impressions or move into positions 4–40. Replace terms with no impressions only after checking Search Console wording and whether QuizBall has a genuinely relevant landing page.
