# Authority pitch briefs — drafts only

These are internal starting points. They are intentionally not addressed to a
person and must not be sent until the owner approves the target, current data and
final wording.

## Brief 1 — independent football publication

**Possible subject:** Data idea: what career-path clues football fans remember

**Best fit:** These Football Times, The Sweeper, World Soccer

**Story promise:** A transparent, anonymised look at whether supporters recognise
players more readily from clubs, nationality, achievements or full career paths.

**Evidence required before send:**

- exact reporting period;
- total quiz starts, questions answered and completions;
- country sample sizes;
- three findings that survive a minimum-sample check;
- methodology and limitations link.

**Draft:**

> I work on QuizBall, a football knowledge game. We have been analysing
> anonymised aggregate answers to understand which kinds of clues supporters
> actually recognise — without identifying individual players or presenting a
> small sample as representative.
>
> One finding may fit your coverage: [INSERT VERIFIED FINDING AND SAMPLE]. We can
> provide the underlying aggregate CSV, methodology, limitations and a fresh cut
> for [PUBLICATION'S AUDIENCE]. There is no requirement to feature the game; the
> useful story is the football-memory pattern itself.
>
> Would a short data brief be useful? I can send the three strongest findings and
> chart-ready numbers in the body of the email.

## Brief 2 — Liverpool or Everton community

**Possible subject:** A reader challenge built around [Liverpool/Everton] careers

**Best fit:** This Is Anfield, GrandOldTeam

**Story promise:** An original, unranked challenge that readers can complete
without creating an account, accompanied by a short explanation of the verified
career data used.

**Evidence required before send:**

- confirm every player and club spell against reliable sources;
- keep the challenge outside ranked matchmaking;
- verify mobile performance and no signup wall;
- create publication-specific copy rather than reusing an SEO page.

**Draft:**

> I work on QuizBall and noticed that our [Liverpool/Everton] career and club
> questions are among the strongest-engagement topics on the site. Rather than
> asking you to promote a generic game, we would like to prepare a self-contained
> [CLUB] reader challenge for your editorial review: ten verified career paths,
> no signup wall and a short results summary your readers can discuss.
>
> If that format is useful, I can first send the complete question list, sources
> and proposed introduction. Nothing would be published or branded as a
> partnership without your approval.

## Brief 3 — Spanish or Mexican publication

**Possible subject:** Propuesta de datos: qué pistas de fútbol reconocen mejor los aficionados

**Best fit:** Panenka, Revista Líbero, Futbol Total, Get Spanish Football News

**Story promise:** A Spanish-language, market-specific snapshot built only after
Spain or Mexico has a defensible sample.

**Evidence required before send:**

- native review of the Spanish copy;
- Spain and Mexico reported separately;
- comparative claims require, per market and within the same reporting window,
  at least 100 relevant quiz starts, 800 eligible answered questions and 40
  completed quizzes;
- define the comparison denominator before analysis as correct eligible answers
  divided by all eligible answers for the stated clue type, excluding skipped
  and unserved questions, and publish the numerator and denominator;
- local player/club examples and source verification;
- no claim that Spanish speakers are one homogeneous audience.

**Draft:**

> Trabajo en QuizBall, un juego de conocimiento futbolístico disponible en
> español. Estamos analizando respuestas agregadas y anónimas para entender qué
> tipos de pistas —escudos, trayectorias, nacionalidad o logros— reconocen mejor
> los aficionados de [ESPAÑA/MÉXICO].
>
> El dato que podría encajar con su audiencia es [INSERTAR HALLAZGO,
> PERIODO Y MUESTRA VERIFICADOS]. Podemos compartir el CSV agregado, la
> metodología, las limitaciones y un corte específico para vuestro mercado. La
> propuesta es el hallazgo editorial, no una petición de publicar un enlace.
>
> ¿Os serviría un resumen breve con los tres resultados más claros y gráficos
> listos para revisar?

## Approval checklist

Before any approach, the owner must approve:

1. target and named/public contact route;
2. exact subject and body;
3. every number and its reporting window;
4. destination asset and tracking parameters;
5. whether one follow-up is allowed.
