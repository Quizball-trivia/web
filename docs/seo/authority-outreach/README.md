# QuizBall authority outreach

This folder is a review queue, not a sending list. Every target was checked against
an official publication or contact page on 2026-08-30. No one has been contacted.

## Objective

Earn independent, editorially justified mentions of QuizBall by offering useful
football knowledge data, original story angles and relevant playable examples.
The goal is not to buy links, trade links or submit QuizBall to bulk directories.

## First wave to review

Start with six tailored approaches after the owner approves the exact recipient
and copy:

1. These Football Times — a methodology-led story using the Football Knowledge Index.
2. The Sweeper — surprising differences in football knowledge across countries.
3. Panenka — a Spanish football-culture angle, once the Spanish sample is meaningful.
4. Futbol Total — a Mexico-specific `adivina el jugador` insight, after enough local data.
5. This Is Anfield — an original Liverpool knowledge feature or reader challenge.
6. GrandOldTeam — an original Everton knowledge feature or reader challenge.

The first wave deliberately mixes attainable independent publishers with strong
club communities. Larger publications remain in the queue until the story and
sample are strong enough.

## Assets we can offer

- [Football Knowledge Index](https://quizball.io/en/football-knowledge-index)
- [Spanish Football Knowledge Index](https://quizball.io/es/football-knowledge-index)
- [Downloadable aggregate dataset](https://quizball.io/data/quizball-football-knowledge-index-2026.csv)
- [English facts and brand resources](https://quizball.io/en/press)
- [Spanish facts and brand resources](https://quizball.io/es/press)
- A publication-specific, unranked reader quiz built from verified questions
- An anonymised club or country cut of aggregate results, only when the sample is large enough

Before any pitch, refresh every number from production analytics and add the
sample period, sample size and limitations. Never present a tiny sample as a
population-wide finding.

## Scoring

Targets are ordered using four practical checks:

- Editorial relevance: 40%
- Clear, official contact or contribution route: 25%
- Priority-market and audience fit: 20%
- Realistic chance of earning a cited editorial mention: 15%

`A` means prepare a tailored pitch first. `B` means pitch only after a stronger
dataset, timely hook or warm introduction. `C` means community participation only
after moderator/editor approval; never drop promotional links.

## Guardrails

- Owner approval is required before every send, form submission, direct message or post.
- Do not contact founders, team members, Betsson, Plug and Play or GITA.
- Do not ask for a followed link, offer payment for editorial coverage or use a fake persona.
- Disclose QuizBall affiliation immediately.
- One relevant follow-up at most, seven to ten working days later.
- Stop on a decline, no-contact request or moderation rejection.
- Record the outcome and URL in `targets.csv`; do not create duplicate approaches.
- Use Resend only for owner reports unless the owner separately approves a specific outreach send.

## Measurement

For each approved wave, record:

- pitches approved, sent and replied to;
- independent mentions and referring domains earned;
- referral visitors and quiz starts from each mention;
- assisted account creation where attribution is reliable;
- branded-search and AI-citation movement over 28 days.

Judge outreach on relevant mentions and qualified visitors, not email-open rate.
